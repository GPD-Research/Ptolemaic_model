# Chapter PT-1: Aristotelian Foundations of the Cosmos

Before calculating planetary positions, the Ptolemaic model requires accepting the physical axioms of the classical era, largely established by Aristotle. In this framework, physics is determined by an object's "nature" and its location in the cosmic hierarchy.

## The Sublunary Sphere
Everything below the orbit of the Moon is composed of four elements: Earth, Water, Air, and Fire. 
* The natural motion of these elements is **rectilinear** (straight lines).
* Earth and Water naturally fall toward the center of the universe.
* Air and Fire naturally rise away from the center.
* Because Earth is the heaviest element and has settled at the center of the universe, the Earth is strictly immobile.

## The Celestial Sphere
Everything from the Moon outward is composed of a fifth element: **Aether** (Quintessence).
* Aether is weightless, incorruptible, and eternal.
* Its natural motion is **uniform and circular**, representing geometric perfection.

Therefore, the challenge of Ptolemaic astronomy was to explain the highly irregular observed motions of the planets (which speed up, slow down, and occasionally reverse direction) using *only* combinations of perfect, uniform circular motions.
