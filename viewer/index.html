Dashed line: the equant line, along which the epicycle centre sweeps equal angles in equal times.
Schematic spacing gives every body its own ring and keeps Ptolemy's ratios within each body. Planetary Hypotheses uses his absolute nesting distances in Earth radii (zoom in for the inner bodies).
3-D view: the planets' plane is the ecliptic, inside the sphere of the fixed stars. The zodiac signs (♈︎ …) are measured from the spring equinox and stay put; the stars, and the zodiac constellations with them, drift east by precession. The blue circle is the celestial equator, tilted 23°51′ (Ptolemy's value).
Positions are Ptolemy's predictions extrapolated from his epoch, not the modern sky: they run about 1° behind the real sky in his day and drift to about 10° behind today because his year is slightly too long.
