Credits and sources

  • Star catalogue: F. Verbunt & R. H. van Gent, “The star catalogues of Ptolemaios and Ulugh Beg”, A&A 544, A31 (2012), CDS J/A+A/544/A31, after G. J. Toomer, Ptolemy's Almagest (1984). Modern positions: ESA Hipparcos catalogue (1997).

  • Planetary Hypotheses distances: Ptolemy, Planetary Hypotheses Book I, as edited by B. R. Goldstein, “The Arabic version of Ptolemy's Planetary Hypotheses”, Trans. Am. Phil. Soc. 57.4 (1967).
